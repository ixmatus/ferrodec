## Not swept by S2 (reader A5's uncovered list)

- Decimal32: nothing (rung 1 decides the whole d32 space; ADR-0033/0034 exhaustive tables cover the 18 unary functions and sqrt).
- Decimal64: only exp, sin, cos; tan, ln, log10, pow, the hyperbolics, the inverse trig, the D1 to D4 groups, and the pi family rest on the planted and frozen corpora.
- Decimal128 trig: decades below 15 (small and moderate arguments) are outside S2; the main Arb corpus, the anchor bands, and S1 cover them.
- Decimal128 exp: decades below -20 (where exp(x) hugs 1) are the ADR-0050/0051 anchor band territory, not S2.
- Decimal128 sinh/cosh: decades below -7 (anchor hugging) excluded by design.
- Decimal128 pow: no body stratum at all; edge only.
- Never swept at any format: exp2, exp10, expm1 and the base variants, log2, logp1 family, cbrt, rootn, rsqrt, hypot, compound, powi, powr, asin, acos, atan, atan2, asinh, acosh, atanh, and the seven pi-family functions. Their budgets rest on their derivations plus six planted rows each (ADR-0059 S3) and the frozen Arb corpus.

### A4-1 evidence
Code path: `ExtNum for ExtendedDyn::reduce_trig` (extended_dyn.rs:1397) → `argred::reduce_dyn` (argred.rs:1012); fraction width `frac_dyn = p + 33` (argred.rs:880-882), truncated at 10^-(p+33) absolute (:1117-1118), multiplied by the generated π/2 in `make_residual_dyn` (:960); `sincos_extended_body` runs Taylor; `round_guarded` compares against `budget.dynamic(p)`. Derivation: the extracted fraction carries absolute error in [0, 10^-(p+33)] plus at most 2·10^-(p+36); at P1B-1's worst depth (|y| = 1.317e-38) that is a relative error of 7.6·10^4·10^-p at every p; the constant 33 does not grow with p, so for any input deeper than 30 digits the residual never reaches 10^-(p+3) at any width. In predicate ulps the window term is at most 1.57·10^(-34-e_r) ulp (e_r the decade of |r|), independent of p, at most 1.57e4 for sin and cos (|r| < 10^-37), up to 3.66e4 for tan near an odd multiple. `sin_budget_dyn(p) = 10(6p + 80)` covers 1.57e4 plus its own items only from p ≥ 280; of the widths the driver reaches (220, 440, 880, …) exactly p = 220 is unsound. Reproducer (from scratchpad/A4/tc): `cargo test --offline --features trig,unbounded-ladder --lib a4_sincos_dump -- --nocapture | grep '^SC' > ../scdump.txt; /usr/bin/python3 ../check_sc.py ../scdump.txt`; decided by mpmath at 8,300 dps. Lines: `SCD220 q=4639 neg=False |val|~2.07e-38 err=-1.513e+4 ulp budget=14000 OVER`; `SCD220 neg=True err=1.513e+4 OVER`; `SCD440 err=-7621 budget=27200`; `SCD880 err=-1.189e+4 budget=53600`; `SC2 err=-137.7 budget=10000`. Residual alone (relative units of 10^-p): RD220 −7.31e4, RD440 −3.68e4, RD880 −5.75e4, matching the 7.6e4 bound; `k mod 4` correct on all 161 rows. Refinement of P1B-1: in predicate ulps the rung 2 SIN worst case is ≈ 1.57e4 + 740, 1.6× the 10,000 budget (not ~8×), and applies only to inputs with |r| < 10^-37 (among the per-exponent minima the single q = 4639 input; measured 137.7 ulp there; largest rung 2 error measured over the four deepest inputs 1,381 ulp). The rung 2 overrun is derived; the rung 3 overrun is witnessed. No wrong value (probability ~10^-180). Why missed: argred's own tests use a depth 33.55 input; every corpus checks the delivered format value (a Tier 1 overrun of 10^4 ulp at 220 digits is ~10^182 ulp below what those rows resolve); the `force_rung3` lane is release-only, local-only, a byte identity check at the format width; `ladder_audit` is vacuous in unbounded builds; `dynamic_budgets_track_the_rung2_catalog` pins only the ratio to the mis-sized rung 2 constant.

## Design notes
1. One shared cancellation depth constant per format (Decimal128 38, Decimal64 20, Decimal32 10), pinned by a committed probe, feeding `FRAC_DIGITS`, `FRAC2_DIGITS`, `frac_digits_dyn` (the literal 33 appears in more than 8 places).
2. State budgets in ulps where a residual can be small (the relative-to-ulp conversion varies by up to 10× with the leading digits).
3. Run `force_rung3` in debug on a corpus slice.
4. Separate "entry" from "Ziv doubling" counters; a pinned doubling count is the natural tripwire for the p = 220 budget.

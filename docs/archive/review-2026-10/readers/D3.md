### Evidence

**D3-1.** 0033:160-164 reads: "half ULP margin at Decimal128 sits at most 10^-34; cumulative kernel error ... roughly 10^-46 ... 10^12 of headroom against the format's half ULP." Bounding the error by the size of a half ULP only proves faithful rounding; correct rounding needs it below the distance to the boundary, which has no lower bound. 0033:218-221 builds on this to call the unscanned 6000 decades "a coverage gap rather than a correctness gap". ADR-0059 Outcome, 0059:337-346, records 1,819 Arb certified misrounds in exactly that band. 0033:3 marks only ADR-0050, and 0059:500-502 calls 0033 "builds on". Why the stack missed it: no instrument reads ADR bodies or cross marks. The ADR-0052 guard checks references only.

**D3-2.** The honesty amendment at 0032:130-143 concedes the d128 trig discharge is empirical, yet still ends "The bound holds across every decade the table covers". The record disagrees with itself: testing.md:146-150 and README.md:204 state the S1 refutation, while testing.md:446-449, README.md:301 and exp.rs:40-47 still present the ADR-0032 envelope as the mechanism. Same blind spot as D3-1.

**D3-3.** `git show 31f1ac8` contains: "sticks_to, a snap test that fires when a result lies within ~1e-47 relative ... the reason a bare equality test is unsound: atanh's log1p composition leaves side-unreliable noise around x". ADR-0059:137-141 and 476 refer to the snap threshold without amending 0051. The code's separation argument (extended.rs:339-364) looks sound to me; the record is what is wrong. The test is mirrored on every rung (extended.rs:1093, extended2.rs:346, extended_dyn.rs:899).

**D3-4.** ADR-0029:44-56 decides to remove or reserve bare `rem` and the `%` impl. The impls are at src/ops/ops_traits.rs:59-68 (`rem_near`) and at line 59 of the d64 and d32 `src/ops_traits.rs` files (`rem_trunc`). The CHANGELOG [2.0.0] entry says the impls "are retained (so `impl num_traits::Num` continues to hold)". Bare `rem` was removed correctly. The hazard ADR-0027 named, the same operator doing different math per type, is still live behind the `ops` feature.

**D3-6.** The exact INEXACT logic is in src/convert/binary.rs:103-105 and 140-155. ADR-0056:158 says "the family converges" but covers only the fixed-width formats.

**D3-12.** Command: `python3 -c "for P in (7,16,34): print(P,[k for k in range(1,200) if len(str(5**k))==P+1])"` Output: `7 [11]`, `16 [23, 24]`, `34 [49, 50]`. Oracle: exact integer arithmetic. `2^-k = 5^k·10^-k` is a nearest-mode midpoint exactly when `5^k` has P+1 digits.

## Least sure

- Adjudicator panic. The ADR-0060 adjudicator panics on `Equal` (ladder.rs:481). Any gap in the algebraic group's classifiers would therefore be a public-API panic in powi, rootn, compound, rsqrt or hypot rather than a misround. Lead for the correctness readers.
- Huge precision panic. ferrodec-decimal/src/arith.rs:248 has a `u32::try_from(...).expect` that panics when precision is within 1 of `u32::MAX`. That precision is legal under `NonZeroU32`, though practically unreachable.
- NaN conversion doc. The `to_f64` rustdoc says NaN passes through "bit-exactly", but both implementations return `f64::NAN`, dropping sign and payload (binary.rs:126, to_float.rs:47). IEEE 754 §6.3 does not constrain the NaN sign here, so this is a doc overclaim only.
- Sibling `trig-pi` features imply `binary-float` (d64 Cargo.toml:76, d32 Cargo.toml:72), which sits oddly with ADR-0061's minimal-feature pitch. Unchecked.
- Unreconciled rates. The Tier 2 figure (0059:191-193), the rung 2 ambiguity rate of ~10^-66 per call (0060:209) and the per-call restatements do not obviously agree: 10^-66 × 10^38 ≈ 10^-28. Not derived.
- Not re-derived or re-measured: ADR-0060's floors; `liouville_probe.py`; the cost and size figures in ADR-0059 and ADR-0061; the ADR-0056 boundary unit tests; the ADR-0048 D64 35 / D32 0 counts against the runner pins.
- Out of scope: ADRs 0001-0049 outside the ten were swept for status and Related lines only.

## Design notes

1. Machine-checked ADR `relations:` frontmatter, enforced by the ADR-0052 guard pattern, would have caught D3-1, D3-7, D3-8 and D3-9.
2. Plans are called snapshots but carry status lines. Pick one home for status.
3. The template needs a "Decision falsified, kept for history" state. 0045's banner is the precedent; 0032 and 0033 need one now.
4. A test asserting that the per-crate constants agree, or hoisting them into ferrodec-ieee, would turn the 0057 drift risk into a check.
5. Test-only instruments are split between Cargo features and `--cfg` flags. Unifying on cfg keeps the published feature surface honest.

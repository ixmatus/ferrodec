# Reader B3: the I/O boundary on all three formats (Wave 3)

Lens: attacker supplied strings and bytes. Name the threat model
first: who can supply this input and what is the worst they can do
(panic, unbounded work, a wrong value that parses back differently,
a non canonical encoding that escapes into arithmetic).

## Scope (yours alone in this wave)

- Root `src/convert/{parse,format,binary,int,mod}.rs`, `src/dpd.rs`,
  `src/serde_impls.rs`, `src/num_traits_impls.rs`, `src/ops_traits.rs`,
  `src/iter.rs` (about 4,900 lines) and their d64 and d32 twins
  (mirror sizes: parse 597/604, format 1,138/1,139, binary
  1,069/1,110, int 715/740, dpd 554/792 changed lines). B2 classifies
  the other twins; you classify these.

Governing: §3.5 (non canonical encodings read as zero or canonical
NaN), §5.12 (conversion to and from external character sequences;
the precision and exponent range of the string form), §6.2 (NaN
payloads and signs), §5.8 (conversion to integer formats: INVALID
versus INEXACT), ADR-0055/0056 (`from_f64`, `to_f64` and the
"deliberate under report"), ADR-0057 (saturation sentinel), ADR-0058
(engineering notation), ADR-0027/0029 (`%`), decTest `dqEncode`,
`ddEncode`, `dsEncode`, `dqToSci`, `dqToEng`, `dqToIntegral`, and
the June report's confirmed finding 3 (NaN payload canonicality in
`[10^33, 2^110)`).

## Checklist

1. **`parse_str_inner` at every edge** (about 210 lines, three
   copies): coefficients of 10^4 and 10^5 digits with leading zeros;
   exponent strings beyond `i32` and `i64` (ADR-0057 saturation and
   what `0E+99999999999` becomes, cohort included); the sign of a
   NaN; NaN payload digits past the format's limit (canonicality:
   the root must not accept `[10^33, 2^110)` payloads, June 3);
   signaling NaN spellings; infinity spellings; whitespace, `+` in
   the exponent, a bare `.`, non ASCII bytes, embedded NUL; the
   `from_str_const` panics and messages. Oracle: CPython `decimal`
   with the format's context for value and cohort; decTest
   `dqBase`/`ddBase`/`dsBase` string rows.
2. **`convert/format.rs`** (32 panic, unreachable, expect or unwrap
   sites, the largest hotspot in the workspace): reachability of
   each through `Display`, `to_sci`, `to_eng`, `{:e}`, width and
   precision format specifiers, with every class including non
   canonical encodings reached via `from_bits` (coefficient field
   above `10^P − 1`, non canonical DPD declets, out of range
   exponent fields). ADR-0058 engineering notation at zero with
   every exponent residue mod 3 against `dqToEng`.
3. **`binary.rs`** (`from_f64`, `to_f64`, and the `f32` pair if
   present): INEXACT decided exactly on the root since fd-aqs.12;
   NaN payload mapping and the doc overclaim (D3 lead); subnormal
   `f64` inputs decoded exactly; `to_f64` at ties and under directed
   modes; the "deliberate under report" of ADR-0056 stated and
   tested; the twins' versions against the root.
4. **`int.rs`**: `to_i64`, `to_u64`, `to_i128`, the rounding
   variants, saturation versus INVALID per §5.8 (`convertToInteger`
   signals INVALID on out of range and NaN, INEXACT on fractions
   only in the `Exact` forms); `from_i128` rounding past 34 digits;
   the Phase 0 `debug_assert_eq!` at `src/convert/int.rs:327` (what
   it asserts and whether release code relies on it).
5. **`dpd.rs`** against the `*Encode` vectors and the declet
   boolean equations: the eight non canonical declet patterns decode
   per the spec table and re-encode canonically; the d32 twin
   diverges by 792 changed lines, say why.
6. **`serde_impls.rs`**: the representation under self describing
   and non self describing formats (string, bits, or both), and the
   bound on work when deserializing attacker bytes; round trip of
   NaN payloads, signs of zero, non canonical inputs.
7. **`num_traits_impls.rs`**: `Num::from_str_radix` with radix ≠ 10
   (error, panic or silent decimal parse), `FromPrimitive` and
   `ToPrimitive` semantics versus `int.rs`, `Zero`/`One` identities
   on cohorts, `Signed` on NaN and `−0`, `Bounded`.
8. **`ops_traits.rs` and `iter.rs`**: the operator defaults (which
   rounding mode, whether status is dropped); `%` routing per format
   with the rustdoc that cites ADR-0027 falsely (D3-4, three
   copies): what `%` computes on each crate; `Neg` on NaN
   (sign flip without INVALID); `Sum` and `Product` accumulation
   order and status.
9. **Twin classification** for the five convert files, `dpd.rs`,
   and the trait files: width, deliberate with citation, or defect.

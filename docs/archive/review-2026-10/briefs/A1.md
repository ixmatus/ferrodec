# Reader A1: the exact and tie classifiers (Wave 2)

Lens: correctness of `ferrodec-transcend/src/exact.rs` (3,800 lines)
and `exact_pi.rs` (559). The ADR-0059 claim rests on two premises;
classification completeness is yours. A wrong `Some` ships a wrong
value; a wrong `None` sends an exact value into the ladder, where the
adjudicator panics on `Equal` and the fixed rungs deliver a rounded
value with INEXACT where the result was exact.

## Scope (yours alone in this wave)

- `ferrodec-transcend/src/exact.rs`, `exact_pi.rs`.
- The Phase 1 splice artifact `docs/archive/review-2026-10/splice/`
  (six D3 agent working trees, each a `git diff HEAD` plus untracked
  files): the merged `exact.rs` was hand spliced from these.

Governing: ADR-0059 (M7 classification leg), ADR-0060 (Engine A and B,
the operand ranges), ADR-0061 (Niven closure, no ties), ADR-0047
(exact flag), the Lauter and Lefèvre registry entry for the `pow`
boundary, the Phase 1b notes §3 and §4.

## Method

1. **Dossier first.** For every public classifier (`*_exact_input`,
   `*_exact_parts`, `*_exact_or_tie`, `log*_exact`, `exp*_integer`,
   `*_preferred_quantum`, the pi family's tables): its claimed theorem
   in one sentence, its width and range gate constants with line
   numbers, and every bail site's stated proof verbatim with its line.
   Return the dossier as a section of the report; it is the artifact
   the coordinator reads the code against.
2. **Attack value soundness and completeness** with constructed
   inputs on all three formats and all five modes against
   `fractions.Fraction` (and CPython `decimal` for the format
   rounding): exact values at the width gate edge and one past it;
   ties at `PRECISION + 1` digits in every family that has them;
   operands with trailing zeros and with negative exponents; results
   in the subnormal range and outside the exponent range; `pow` at
   the Lauter Lefèvre criterion boundary; `compound` at `|n|·w` = 196
   and 197 and the nines patterns at both ends of the exponent range;
   `hypot` with operands decades apart and the `S = k² + 1` family;
   `rootn` at every `n` in range with perfect powers and near misses;
   `rsqrt` of exact reciprocals of squares; `log2p1` and `log10p1`
   nines families at the digit width limit; the pi family at the
   residue class edges. Structured coefficients matter (C2-1 was a
   power of ten the random strategy never drew): powers of ten, all
   nines, `5·10^k`, and their neighbours.
3. **Splice check.** For each agent capture under `splice/`, compare
   every function body it delivered for `exact.rs` against the merged
   file: a body lost, truncated, or interleaved with another's is a
   finding. Memory records that the three way splice once ate byte
   identical constant bodies.
4. **The witness tests.** C1-13 found that the `cbrt` and `pow`
   witness tests never call the classifier they name. Call the
   classifiers directly from your scratch crate (they are
   `pub(crate)`; use a test module inside a copy only if you must, and
   say so), never through the kernel.
5. **The `Equal` panic.** For each operation the adjudicator serves,
   construct the exact boundary inputs (grid points and midpoints)
   and confirm the classifier delivers them before the kernel; any
   that reach the ladder are a public API panic.

Severity guide: a wrong `Some` is P0; a wrong `None` that reaches a
guarded delivery is P1 (INEXACT on an exact result, or a directed
mode off by one) and P0 if it reaches the adjudicator.

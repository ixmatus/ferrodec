# Reader B2: the Decimal64 and Decimal32 siblings, side by side (Wave 3)

Lens: every divergence between the root and the two siblings is
either width parameterization, deliberate with a citation, or a
suspected defect; and every sibling wrapper's public contract matches
the kernel it calls.

## Scope (yours alone in this wave)

- `ferrodec-decimal64/src/` and `ferrodec-decimal32/src/` less
  `convert/` (B3) and `verify/` (C2, done): the core modules and
  `ops/*` (about 8,800 and 9,200 lines), read side by side with the
  root using the Phase 1 mirror diffs; the ten sibling transcendental
  wrappers `ops/{exp,hyper,trig,trig_pi,pow,powr,rootn,rsqrt,hypot,
  compound}.rs` (2,268 lines per crate) against the root `src/math/*`
  shims; `transcend_impl.rs` against the root `format_impl.rs`.
- The sibling `tests/` only where a wrapper's test pins the behaviour
  you judge (C1 and C2 have read the suites).

Governing: ADR-0011 (three crates), ADR-0030 (subnormal single
rounding), ADR-0057 (the saturation sentinel duplicated per crate),
ADR-0029 (the 2.0 breaking set), ADR-0059/0061 (the kernels the
wrappers call and their tiers), the June report's confirmed finding 2
(the d64 `POW10_U64` class), `wave1-verification.md` C2-2 to C2-7 and
`wave2-verification.md` A5-4, A5-5, A5-10.

## Checklist

1. **Divergence classification, every pair.** Regenerate the diffs
   (`mirror_diff.sh`) and classify every hunk of root versus d64,
   root versus d32, and d64 versus d32 for the modules in scope. The
   large ones first: `ops/fma.rs` (1,722 and 1,850 changed lines),
   `ops/quantum.rs` (1,477), `ops/addsub.rs` (1,160), `cmp.rs`
   (1,014), `classify.rs` (928), `ops/round.rs` (966 and 1,055),
   `ops/rem.rs` (945 and 1,042), `ops/div.rs`, `ops/mul.rs`,
   `ops/sqrt.rs`, `bid.rs`, `dpd.rs` (B3 owns dpd; note only). A
   divergence that is neither width nor cited is a finding with the
   root as the reference unless the sibling is the one that is right
   (say which, with the oracle).
2. **Tables and constants sized per format.** Every `POW10`, digit
   count, shift and threshold table in both siblings against the
   format's width (the June `POW10_U64` class); the constants pinned
   by value, as B1 does for the root.
3. **ADR-0030 and the subnormal band.** Single rounding on every
   path that produces a subnormal result in d64 and d32 (add, mul,
   div, fma, sqrt, quantize, scaleb, the transcend wrappers'
   deliveries), with UNDERFLOW and INEXACT per §7.5, against the
   oracle.
4. **The three way special case divergence.** `pow(sNaN, ±0)` is
   `1` with INVALID on the root and `1` with no flag on d64 (C2-3,
   A5-5); `pow(±0, −∞)` raises DIV_BY_ZERO on all three through
   separate copies (A5-4; the sibling copy is
   `ferrodec-decimal64/src/ops/pow.rs:161`); `atan2(qNaN, sNaN)`
   raises no INVALID on the siblings (C2-2); sibling `min`/`max`
   drop the payload (C2-4). For every binary operation in both
   siblings (arithmetic and transcendental), tabulate the dispatch
   order (signaling check before or after the quiet NaN return, zero
   and infinity rows) against the root, and against §7.2 and §9.2.1
   as the crate quotes them. Do not re-report the four known rows;
   complete the table.
5. **Gate constants per format.** `transcend_impl.rs` supplies
   `exp_overflow_limit`, `exp_underflow_limit` and any other gate
   the kernels read. For each, the true boundary from `ln(k·MAX)` or
   `ln(½·MIN_SUBNORMAL)` (mpmath), the constant, the side it errs
   on, and whether a mode aware delivery depends on it. Known: d128
   underflow gate is wrong (A5-2); d64 "≈ −916.98" comment is wrong
   but the gate 918 is safe; d32 gate 235 against −233.254; the
   `sinh`/`cosh` fix (A5-1) will need `ln(2·MAX)` per format
   (887.188, 224.044): state what the siblings must supply.
6. **Wrapper contracts.** Each of the ten wrappers' rustdoc against
   the kernel's actual tier (ADR-0059: correctly rounded at
   Decimal128 under the model; what does the doc claim for d64 and
   d32, and what do the kernels deliver there given the Decimal32
   Newton findings A3-1 and A2-2); the "faithful, ≤ 1 ULP" sentences
   (d64 `ops/exp.rs:3-13`, D2); the §9.2.1 tables the wrappers carry
   locally versus the kernel's `*_special_cases`.
7. **Panic hotspots.** d64 `ops/quantum.rs` (36 panic, unreachable,
   expect or unwrap sites) and `ops/rem.rs` (23); d32 the same files:
   reachability from the public API with legal operands.
8. **The d32 removal of `ExponentOutOfRange`** and every other API
   asymmetry between the three crates in scope (names, `Status`
   semantics, preferred exponents, `reduce`, `logb`, `scaleb`
   ranges): list them; D1 judges the architecture, you supply the
   facts.
9. **Structured differential on the siblings.** The oracle on
   `add`, `sub`, `mul`, `div`, `fma`, `sqrt`, `rem`, `quantize` at
   d64 and d32 with the B1 structured operand families (C2-10 says no
   such suite exists in tree); the C2-1 shape on the sibling fma at
   both widths; counts per operation.

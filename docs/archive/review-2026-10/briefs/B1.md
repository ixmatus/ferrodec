# Reader B1: the root `ferrodec` core (Wave 3)

Lens: correctness of the Decimal128 arithmetic pipelines from decode
to pack, in every mode, with cohort and every flag, against the
validated exact oracle and libmpdec; then the Wave 2 defect classes
applied to this code.

## Scope (yours alone in this wave)

- `src/{bid,classify,decimal,digits,cmp,status,multiword}.rs` and
  `src/ops/{mod,addsub,mul,div,divide_integer,fma,rem,sqrt,round,
  integral,quantum,reduce,logical,rotate,shift,nan_propagate}.rs`
  (about 10,400 lines). Excludes `src/convert/` (B3), `src/math/`
  (A5, done), `src/verify/` (C2, done) and the siblings (B2).

Governing: IEEE 754-2019 §5, §6.3 (cohorts of exact results), §7
(flags), §9.2.2 as the crate reads it (`docs/` and the fd-5g6 rules);
ADR-0030 (subnormal single rounding), ADR-0047/0048 (flag fidelity,
CLAMPED and the BID residual), ADR-0057 (saturation sentinel),
ADR-0027/0029 (`rem`), the June report's confirmed findings 1 and 2
(constants and tables).

## Checklist

1. **The fma binade class, widened.** With C2's driver shape
   (`reproducers/P1B-3`): exact products or sums of 35 or more
   digits that are a power of ten or all nines, with a far addend of
   either sign, every mode, through `add`, `sub`, `fma`, `mul`
   (exact product at the P+1 digit boundary), `div` and `sqrt`
   (exact quotients and roots at the boundary); then the same at the
   subnormal edge and at `Emax`. Oracle: `oracle.rs` and libmpdec
   together (value, cohort, five flags, CLAMPED).
2. **Zero results.** The property suites exclude zeros. Exact zero
   sums of opposite sign operands (sign per mode under §6.3: `−0`
   only under TowardNegative), exact zero products and quotients
   (cohort `Q(x)+Q(y)`, `Q(x)−Q(y)` clamped into range), `fma` with a
   zero product and a zero addend, `sqrt(−0)`, `rem` returning zero
   (sign of the dividend), `quantize` to a zero, `reduce` of a zero
   (cohort kept or canonicalised, and what the doc says), `round`
   and `integral` of tiny values to zero (sign, INEXACT, and the
   §5.9 rule that `roundToIntegral` signals nothing but
   `roundToIntegralExact` signals INEXACT). All five modes.
3. **Exponent and width arithmetic.** The 372 `as` casts in the
   root: every cast in an exponent, shift count, digit count or limb
   index with its bound; `i128` preferred exponent arithmetic and its
   clamp into `[Emin − P + 1, Emax − P + 1]`; `rem` and
   `divide_integer` with exponent gaps near 12,000 (loop bounds,
   `alloc` free, termination, the §5.7.1 "integer part too large"
   INVALID for `divide_integer` and the quotient digit count).
4. **Constants pinned by value.** List every named constant in scope
   (`MIN_POSITIVE_NORMAL`, `MAX`, `EPSILON`, the exponent bounds,
   the digit tables, `POW10` tables and their lengths) and the test
   that reads its value, not its identity. June's finding 1 was a
   constant no test read; the Wave 1 C1 trust table shows the same
   shape in the transcend tests.
5. **CLAMPED and the BID residual (ADR-0048).** Results whose
   preferred exponent exceeds `Emax − P + 1` with trailing zeros
   (clamp fires, CLAMPED set, value unchanged) versus the case where
   the clamp would need digits that are not zero (OVERFLOW instead);
   the BID encoding's own clamp of the exponent field; `quantize`
   and `scaleb` at the clamp edge; the oracle models no CLAMPED, so
   use libmpdec with `clamp=1`.
6. **`rem` and `rem_near`.** Ties in `rem_near` (fd-pvu), signs of
   zero remainders, `rem` of a finite by an infinity, the INVALID
   rows, and `%`'s routing (B3 owns the operator trait; you own what
   the method it calls does).
7. **`ops/quantum.rs`** (14 panic sites in production source):
   reachability of each from the public API with legal operands
   (`quantize` to an exponent outside the range, `same_quantum` on
   NaN, `scaleb` by huge integers, `logb` of zero and infinity).
8. **`nan_propagate.rs` and `cmp.rs`.** Payload and sign survival
   per §6.2.3 for two quiet NaNs, a signaling and a quiet NaN in
   either slot, on every binary operation; `total_order` and
   `total_order_mag` on the full cohort of a value, on both zeros,
   on NaN payload ordering; `min`/`max` and the magnitude pair
   against decTest rows 184-187 (the siblings differ here, C2-4; the
   root is said to be right).
9. **Structured differential.** The validated oracle on all nines,
   powers of ten, `5·10^k`, ULP neighbours of both, both subnormal
   edges, `Emax` edges, for `add`, `sub`, `mul`, `div`, `fma`,
   `sqrt`, `rem`, `rem_near`, `divide_integer`, `quantize`, `round`
   variants, all five modes, cohort and flags compared; libmpdec
   beside it for CLAMPED. Report counts per operation and the
   oracle that decided each.

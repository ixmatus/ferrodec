# Reader A4: argument reduction, constants, the dynamic rung (Wave 2)

Lens: correctness of the three readers of one table and of the rung
that has no table.

## Scope (yours alone in this wave)

- `ferrodec-transcend/src/argred.rs` (1,713), `consts.rs` (496),
  `extended_dyn.rs` (2,284), `telemetry.rs`.
- The Phase 1 artifact row for `extended2` versus `extended_dyn`
  (regenerate with `mirror_diff.sh`).

Governing: ADR-0059 (M6 `reduce_wide`, M8b the dynamic rung), the
Payne and Hanek registry entry, ADR-0032's honesty amendment on the
38 digit `π/2`, the Phase 1b notes §1 (constants and generators are
verified clean; do not redo) and §4 (P1B-1, which is your premise to
carry forward).

## Checklist

1. **The depth premise, rung 2 and rung 3.** P1B-1 established the
   true Decimal128 cancellation depth is 37.88 digits (every exponent
   deeper than 33; `reproducers/P1B-1/cancellation_depth.py`).
   `reduce_wide` keeps 143 fractional digits and the catalog assumes
   110 survive; derive what survives at depth 38 and what the rung 2
   trig budgets should be. For `reduce_dyn`, the window and fraction
   widths are functions of `p`: derive whether the formula keeps the
   residual's relative error below `10^-(p+3)` at depth 38 for every
   `p` the Ziv loop reaches, and whether the doubling can ever land
   on a width where it does not.
2. **Window indexing at the extremes.** `1E+6144`, `9.99…E+6144`,
   the largest subnormal, `1E-6176`, the decade shortcut boundary
   (`decade < -1`), the window empty case, the `i_hi` clip at the
   table's end, and the `I_HI_OFFSET` and `CARRY_GUARD` arithmetic
   in `split_product` (the Phase 0 `debug_assert_eq!` sites). The
   worst case input from P1B-1 and the three exponents at depth 37.
3. **Inputs within one ULP of `k·π/2`** for several `k` at each
   format, including the S1 witness shapes; the residual sign and
   the `rounded_up` branch in `make_residual`; the `k mod 4`
   extraction for both signs.
4. **The 6,408 digit table.** Only digits 1 to 38 are pinned (C1-15);
   the coordinator's generator pins agree with mpmath at five depths.
   Confirm the whole table against `bigconst::two_over_pi_digits`
   and state the sizing argument against the widest rung 2 window at
   `q_max`.
5. **The constants.** The 55 and 115 digit literals are verified
   (Phase 1b §1). Check how each is consumed: `parse_str` narrowing
   to 50 or 110 digits, the `3·π/4` composition in `atan2`, the
   `tan(π/8)` threshold compare, and whether any rung reads a
   constant at the wrong width.
6. **The dynamic rung.** `run3` termination and the depth cap's
   panic; `newton_steps`; `DynArena` borrow discipline and allocation
   growth per doubling; `reduce_dyn` (129 lines) and the generator
   bounds it folds into its error budget; the `telemetry` counters
   after the Phase 0 change (`count_rung3_entry` under
   `force_rung3`). Every hunk of the `extended2` versus
   `extended_dyn` diff classified as width parameterization,
   deliberate, or suspected defect.
7. **The `force_rung3` lane runs in no CI workflow** (C5): read what
   it pins and say what a defect in the dynamic rung would need to
   look like to pass the local battery.

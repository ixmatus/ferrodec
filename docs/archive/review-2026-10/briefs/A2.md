# Reader A2: the algebraic group end to end (Wave 2)

Lens: correctness of `rsqrt`, `hypot`, `powi` (the powering arm of
`pow`), `rootn`, and `compound` from the public wrapper to the
adjudicated delivery, including `adjudicate.rs`. The coordinator has
read the adjudicator independently (Phase 1b §3) and will compare
lists afterwards; you receive a defect brief, not that reading.

## Scope (yours alone in this wave)

- `ferrodec-transcend/src/adjudicate.rs`, `rsqrt.rs`, `hypot.rs`,
  `rootn.rs`, `compound.rs`, and the powering arm of `pow.rs`
  (`powi`, `pown_preferred_quantum`, the `|y| ≤ 256` fast path
  boundary); the root wrappers `src/math/{rsqrt,hypot,rootn,compound,
  pow}.rs` for these operations.
- `ladder::round_adjudicated` and `Boundary` (read `ladder.rs` for
  those only; A3 owns the rest).

Governing: ADR-0060 in full (the floors, Engine A and B, the width
table, the operand ranges, the landing deltas), ADR-0051 (the residual
channel the adjudicated delivery uses), the fd-5g6 preferred exponent
rules (§9.2.2), the Phase 1b notes §3.

## Checklist

1. **The adjudicator.** Re-derive the widths table against `U1024`
   (308 digits) from the actual bounds on `C`, `a`, `c`, `N'`, `S`;
   exponent alignment in `cmp_aligned` with negative exponents and
   the `as u32` shift; the sign conventions (the deciders compare
   magnitudes: show that each kernel maps `Side::Above` to the right
   residual direction for a negative result, odd `n` in `powi` and
   `rootn`); `hypot_side`'s assumption that `hypot_aligned_sum`
   returns `(S, q)` with `y² = S·10^(2q)` exactly; `compound_side`'s
   `1 + x = N'·10^s` construction on both signs and the `d > 198`
   gates. Construct inputs for which each decider returns `None`
   (out of range) and confirm the fallback delivery is the
   unadjudicated budgeted one, not a panic.
2. **The kernels at the range edges.** The P0 class of Wave 1 (seeds
   through the format) was probed at the extremes for these five
   kernels and found clean at the probed points only
   (`reproducers/P1B-2/panic_probe_output.txt`): widen it. Every
   `recip`, `div`, `sqrt` call with an operand that can be huge,
   tiny, subnormal, or exactly the format maximum, on all three
   formats. A subnormal seed carries fewer digits than `PRECISION`:
   derive whether any delivered result depends on more digits than
   the seed has (the fd-k9t shape at the exponent edge).
3. **Special case dispatch order** (C2-2, C2-3): for each binary
   operation here (`hypot`, `compound`, `rootn`, `powi`), every
   combination of signaling NaN, quiet NaN, zero, infinity in either
   operand against §7.2 and §9.2.1, on all three formats; the root
   and the siblings have separate special case code.
4. **Newton rsqrt and the powering arm** at Decimal32: the fixed step
   counts reach 25 digits at rung 1 (Phase 1b §2). For each of the
   five operations, state whether a 25 digit intermediate can change
   a delivered Decimal32 result, given the ADR-0060 floors (which
   bound the true value's distance from every boundary) and the
   adjudicator (which decides exactly at rung 2 but not at rung 1).
5. **The arm that tests never reach** (C1-28): `powi` for `|n| ≥ 7`
   and bases whose powers exceed the precision; construct cases that
   actually run the powering arm and compare with mpmath at 120
   digits in all five modes.
6. **Preferred exponents** (§9.2.2, fd-5g6): `floor(n·Q(x))` for
   `pown`, `−floor(Q(x)/2)` for `rsqrt`, `floor(Q(x)/n)` for `rootn`,
   `floor(n·min(0, Q(x)))` for `compound`, on exact and inexact
   deliveries, including the unattainable preference cases.

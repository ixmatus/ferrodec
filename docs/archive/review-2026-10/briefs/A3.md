# Reader A3: the ladder, the two fixed rungs, and the certification chain (Wave 2)

Lens: budget soundness, the first premise of the ADR-0059 claim, and
the chain of definitions that makes the S2 campaign evidence at all.

## Scope (yours alone in this wave)

- `ferrodec-transcend/src/ladder.rs` (except `round_adjudicated` and
  `Boundary`, which A2 reads), `extended.rs`, `extended2.rs`.
- `ferrodec-campaign/src/margin.rs`; the margin definitions in
  `tools/gen_transcend_vectors.py` (`directed_margin`, `tie_margin`);
  the straddle inequality in `tools/campaign_certify.py` and

- The Phase 1 artifact `mirror_summary.md` row for `extended` versus
  `extended2`; regenerate the diff with
  `docs/archive/review-2026-10/mirror_diff.sh <scratch-dir>`.

Governing: ADR-0059 (the claim ladder, the budget discipline, "where
the proof stands"), ADR-0051, ADR-0060 §the floors, the Phase 1b notes
§2 and §4 (read them: they contain the coordinator's derivations and
one open finding you are asked to complete, not to redo).

## Checklist

1. **The certification chain.** Three margin definitions are asserted
   equal: the generator's, `margin.rs`'s (`grid_x2`, `tie_x2`, with
   `EXT_PRECISION` pinned locally), and `ladder.rs`'s
   `near_rounding_boundary` through `candidate_boundary`. Re-derive
   the arithmetic at normal and subnormal widths (the drop position
   mirrors `round_and_pack_finite`'s single rounding rule), then plant
   one wrong side row through `campaign_certify.py` and confirm it is
   rejected. If the chain has a gap, say exactly which rows of the
   frozen S2 corpus it weakens.
2. **The escalation predicate.** `candidate_boundary` and the
   `BoundaryVerdict` variants: both boundary families, every mode,
   the subnormal edge (a value within budget of the `10^E_MIN` decade
   point), the overflow edge, the all nines carry, the zero working
   value, the tenfold pad. Construct values at each and check the
   verdict against an exact rational computation of the distances.
3. **The budgets, after Phase 1b.** The coordinator verified `sin`,
   `atan`, `compound`, `log10p1` line by line and found the trig
   window term rests on a false premise (P1B-1: 33 leading zeros
   assumed, 38 real at Decimal128). Your job is the other 36: for
   each budget constant, check the itemization's terms against the
   kernel code path (is every rounding counted; is every constant
   multiply's magnitude bound right; is the amplification factor
   right; does the composition inherit the right sub budget), and
   list every budget whose only evidence is its derivation (no
   campaign, no planted rows beyond six). Pay attention to the items
   Phase 1b flagged: `COMPOUND` omits `EXP`'s reduction term; `POW`
   may share the omission; the gate magnitude on the underflow side.
4. **The Tier 2 figure's units** (D3-13): ADR-0059 states an expected
   count over the whole domain per function; four restatements call
   it a per call rate. Derive which quantity follows from the budgets
   and the equidistribution model, with the arithmetic shown, so the
   report can state the claim in one form.
5. **fd-k9t and the seed.** Phase 1b §2 has the Newton table. You
   own the dossier of every `recip`, `div`, `sqrt` call site in the
   kernels (file, line, operand provenance, which rung, which
   format), and two derived questions: (a) at Decimal32 rung 1, which
   delivered results can a 25 digit intermediate change, given each
   function's floor or exhaustive table; (b) the Wave 1 P0 class
   (seeds through `to_format`): for every call site, the operand
   ranges that produce an infinite, zero, or subnormal seed, and
   whether the subnormal case (a seed with fewer digits than
   `PRECISION`) can change a delivered value.
6. **The two rungs as mirrors.** Classify every hunk of the
   `extended` versus `extended2` diff as width parameterization,
   deliberate (citing the comment), or suspected defect; the
   `sticks_to` threshold, the `to_format_with_residual` construction,
   the rounding of the `U384` product, and the subnormal drop in both.
7. **`parse_str` panics** on invalid literals in both rungs: reachable
   from the public API or not (every call site).

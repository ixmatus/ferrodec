# Reader D2: the claims inventory (Wave 1)

Lens: does every checkable sentence in the project's documents say
something true about the tree, the numbers, and the workflows as
they are at this tip? Your output is the contradiction list that the
correctness readers in the next two waves use as falsification
targets, and the corrected text the report's documentation tier
needs.

## Scope (you own these)

- `README.md` (root) and the sibling `README.md` files in
  `ferrodec-decimal64/`, `ferrodec-decimal32/`, `ferrodec-decimal/`,
  and any other crate that has one.
- `docs/testing.md`.
- `KNOWN_ISSUES.md` in root, `ferrodec-decimal64/`,
  `ferrodec-decimal32/`, `ferrodec-decimal/`.
- The `Unreleased` section of every `CHANGELOG.md` in the workspace.
- The landing notes and addenda of ADR-0059, ADR-0060, ADR-0061
  (`docs/decisions/0059-*.md`, `0060-*.md`, `0061-*.md`): only their
  factual claims about what landed and what is open.
- `docs/references/INDEX.md` and `docs/references/verification-map.md`.
- The module level docs (`//!`) of `ferrodec-transcend/src/lib.rs`.
- `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/PLAN.md` (the latter marked superseded: is
  that status line accurate and is anything in it still cited as
  live?).

You do not read the code to find defects; you read it only far
enough to decide whether a sentence about it is true.

## Method

Go sentence by sentence. For each checkable claim produce a row:

| file:line | claim (abbreviated) | verdict | evidence | corrected text |

Verdicts: `true`, `false`, `stale` (was true at some commit, name
it if you can), `unverifiable` (say what would verify it). Corrected
text is your proposed replacement sentence, in the project's prose
style (no hyphens or em dashes; subjects up front).

**The disclosure section.** `README.md` from the heading "How
ferrodec is developed" to the next top level heading is read for
truth exactly like the rest, but you never propose edits to it:
report any false sentence there in a separate section titled
"Disclosure section: proposed diff for Parnell" with the sentence,
the fact, and nothing else. That section is handled by a human.

## Deliverable in addition to the schema

The **claims table** (all rows, including `true` ones, so the
coverage is visible), and a short **contradiction list** of every
`false` and `stale` row sorted by which correctness reader it seeds
(transcend kernels; format crates; decimal; multiword; tooling).

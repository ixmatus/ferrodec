# Reader A5: the per function kernels and the public wrapper layer (Wave 2)

Lens: correctness of the transcendental kernels on every path to a
delivery, in every mode, on every format, with the Wave 1 instrument
blind spots in mind: the faithful oracle is one sided (C1-1), eleven
functions have no directed mode corpus rows below Decimal128 (C1-5),
and the fuzz targets have not run since May.

## Scope (yours alone in this wave)

- `ferrodec-transcend/src/{exp,ln,sincos,sincospi,inverse_trig,
  inverse_trig_pi,hyperbolic,powr,cbrt}.rs` and `pow.rs` less the
  powering arm (A2 owns `powi`); the root wrapper layer
  `src/math/*.rs` (3,018 lines: the public rustdoc theorems, the
  byte identity shims, `format_impl.rs`).
- The Phase 1 artifact `s2_coverage.md` (what S2 swept and what it
  never swept) and the C1 trust table rows for the corpora.

Governing: ADR-0050 (anchor band reformulations), ADR-0051 (residual
seam), ADR-0047 (exact flag), ADR-0059, ADR-0061, the June report's
confirmed findings 3 and 4 (the directed mode and anchor band
families; know them so you do not re-report what was fixed and so you
test the fixes), the Phase 1b notes §4.

## Checklist

1. **The P0 class, completed.** `asinh`, `acosh`, `atan2`, and the
   siblings' `atan2_pi` panic when a seed rounds through the format
   out of range (`reproducers/P1B-2`). Do not re-report. Do find the
   rest: every path in your scope where an intermediate is rounded
   through `to_format` or built by `from_format` of a computed value
   (gates, anchors, saturation proxies, `exp_overflow_limit`
   comparisons), with the operand ranges that make it infinite, zero,
   or subnormal, on all three formats. Then state what the fix for
   the known four should be so that the whole class closes
  (rescale before seeding, or dispatch the ratio first) and which
   kernels need which.
2. **Directed modes and the residual seam, per function and mode.**
   Small argument deliveries across the `to_format` seam (is the
   ADR-0051 residual channel on every path that needs one, with the
   right side theorem); exact result handling and INEXACT fidelity
   (the ADR-0047 extension to `exp2`, `log2`, `log10`, and the Track
   D families); overflow and underflow gates mode aware on every
   kernel that has one (`exp`'s is shared; `sinh`, `cosh`, `tanh`,
   `pow`, `powr`, `compound`'s own); negate after round in every
   odd function and every constant delivery; the `1 ± tiny`
   absorption class (ADR-0050 reformulations present on every path
   that needs one, including the Track D families added after
   ADR-0050). Probe against Arb through the pfloat venv at the June
   anchor bands and at the decades S2 never swept (the list in
   `s2_coverage.md`), in all five modes, and against mpmath at 120
   digits elsewhere. Structured inputs (powers of ten, all nines,
   `5·10^k`) at every gate.
3. **Special case dispatch order** (C2-2, C2-3) for every binary
   operation in scope (`pow`, `powr`, `atan2`, `atan2_pi`) and the
   §9.2.1 tables for every unary one: signaling NaN in either slot
   against §7.2, quiet NaN propagation payload and sign, the signed
   zeros and infinities, on all three formats (the root wrapper
   layer and the siblings' `ops/*.rs` carry separate copies; B2 owns
   the siblings' wrappers, you own the kernels and the root).
4. **The anchor seams' thresholds.** `sticks_to` fires at `10^-47`
   relative; each kernel's anchor gate (`compound_anchor`'s
   `adj ≤ −(P + dn + 4)`, `log10p1_power_of_ten_exponent`'s `n ≥ 36`,
   `exp`'s 1 anchor, the `−1` collapse in `exp10m1`): derive each
   gate's soundness margin and construct inputs just inside and just
   outside.
5. **The `tan` asymptote branch** (D2-3, D2-H1): the coordinator's
   probe shows the reduced fraction is never zero at Decimal128, so
   `cos_ext.is_zero()` is unreachable there; check the siblings and
   the rung 2 and rung 3 readers, and the doc that says `±∞` is
   returned.
6. **The `POW` and `POWR` itemizations** for the omission Phase 1b
   found in `COMPOUND` (the shared `exp` reduction term), and `pow`'s
   `|y| ≤ 256` fast path boundary and its `ONE / result` inversion
   path (a `div_fmt` at the format width: what does it round, and is
   that a second rounding).
7. **The wrapper rustdoc theorems** in `src/math/*.rs` (for example
   `expm1`'s exact set and `trig_pi`'s no ties claim) against the
   kernel; the sibling wrapper docs that still say "faithful" are
   B2's, but the root's are yours.
8. **Blast radius of C1-1.** Pick two functions and two modes, patch
   a copy of the kernel to deliver the next value down under
   TowardPositive (in your scratch crate, never in the repository),
   and report which committed suites would turn red. The prediction
   from Wave 1 is that the property suites stay green and only the
   frozen corpora catch it, where they have directed rows.

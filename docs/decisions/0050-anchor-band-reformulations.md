# ADR-0050: Anchor band reformulations restore the kernel's relative error model

- **Status**: accepted (amends ADR-0032 and ADR-0033)
- **Date**: 2026-06-10

## Context

ADR-0032 commits the §9.2 transcendental surface to correctly rounded
on all three fixed formats, discharged by a proof envelope: the 50
digit Extended kernel's cumulative error, modeled as a small multiple
of `10^-49` *relative* to the result, clears the smallest empirical
Arb worst case half ULP margin by more than thirty orders of
magnitude. ADR-0033 extended the empirical side to an exhaustive
margin search over the full canonical Decimal32 input set and, on the
strength of the same error model, stated the Decimal32 surface proven
correctly rounded at every canonical input.

The 2026-06-09 review (archived at
`docs/archive/REPORT-rigorous-review-2026-06-09.md`) falsified the
error model's implicit lemma in the neighbourhoods of the additive
anchors 0 and 1. Four kernel formulas hand `1 ± tiny` (or a tiny
correction against an absorbed anchor) to the 50 significant digit
working representation. Absorption at the anchor turns the model's
`~1e-49` *relative* error *absolute*, and a result whose magnitude is
far below 1 then carries an error that is enormous relative to its
own ULP. Oracle confirmed against libmpdec and mpmath:

- `atanh` and `asinh` returned exact `+0` for arguments below the
  working resolution (every format; the correct result is the
  argument itself, a normal representable value), and mid band
  results wrong by up to `3e8` ULP at Decimal128; at Decimal64,
  `atanh(1.536385851986827E-44)` was wrong at the sixth significant
  digit.
- `ln`, `log10`, and `log2` just below 1 at Decimal128 were wrong by
  up to `4e7` ULP for arbitrary coefficient inputs: the decade route
  reconstructed a near zero result as `taylor + k·ln 2 − ln 10`, a
  cancellation of ~2 magnitude addends each carrying `~1e-49`
  absolute rounding error. Inputs of the clean form `1 − 10^-k`
  happened to round correctly, which is why spot checks passed.
- `acos` near 1 was wrong by up to `1.5e6` ULP at Decimal128
  (`π/2 − asin(x)` cancels two ~π/2 operands against a `√(2δ)` sized
  result), and `asin` near 1 breached the proof envelope (measured
  0.4 to 0.5 ULP of error against a `1.052e-3` ULP margin) through
  the rounding residue of squaring in `1 − x²`.
- `pow` inherited the `ln` defect through `y · ln(x)` for bases near
  1 with large exponents.

No existing layer sampled these bands: the Arb corpus coefficients
stop at 8 digits and its per function decades stop short of the
anchors; the astro-float property suites assert the strictly weaker
faithful (≤ 1 ULP) contract; the ADR-0033 exhaustive sweep searched
NearestEven margins on the Arb side and executed the kernel at only
the 18 worst margin inputs; decTest has no transcendentals; the
cross precision check routes both sides through the same shared
kernel, so the failure correlated — precisely the hazard
`docs/testing.md` documents as the correlated failure surface.

## Decision

Reformulate the affected derivations so every path's accuracy is
relative to its own result, never absolute against an absorbed
anchor. The cures are the standard cancellation free forms, two of
which (`acosh`'s log1p path, exact factoring) were already in tree:

1. **`ln` near 1** (`ln.rs::ln_from_extended`): for `x ∈ (0.5, 1.5)`
   feed `u = x − 1` (exact at Extended width) straight to the log1p
   series, skipping the decade decomposition and its halve/double
   reconstruction. `log10`, `log2`, and `pow` inherit the repair
   through composition.
2. **`atanh` small band** (`hyperbolic.rs`): for `|x| < 0.15`,
   `atanh(x) = ½·log1p(2x / (1 − x))`; the log1p argument is built
   from `x` directly, so absorption in `1 − x` perturbs it only
   relatively. Above the threshold the ratio form is well
   conditioned and unchanged.
3. **`asinh` small band** (`hyperbolic.rs`): for `|x| < 0.3`,
   `asinh(x) = log1p(|x| + x² / (1 + √(1 + x²)))`, same reasoning.
4. **`asin`** (`inverse_trig.rs::asin_ext`): factor the radicand
   `1 − x² = (1 − |x|)(1 + |x|)`; both factors are exact for format
   sourced coefficients.
5. **`acos`** (`inverse_trig.rs`): replace `π/2 − asin(x)` with
   `acos(x) = 2·atan(√((1 − x)/(1 + x)))` across the whole open
   domain. Both factors are exact at the hazardous ends (`1 − x`
   near 1, `1 + x` near −1) and `atan` preserves relative accuracy
   on both of its branches, so no subtraction anywhere meets a
   result smaller than its operands.

The standing witness is a committed near anchor band corpus,
`tests/vectors/transcend/anchor_bands/` (867 vectors across the
three format precisions), generated by
`tools/gen_anchor_band_vectors.py`: mpmath at 160 dps under a
`1e-90` ULP boundary margin guard, libmpdec cross confirming
`ln`/`log10`/`pow` at NearestEven, with arbitrary ("messy")
coefficients in every hazard decade. The per (function, mode) counts
are pinned exactly in `tests/transcend_anchor_bands.rs` and the two
sibling mirrors, per the repository's regression guard discipline.
Directed mode lines appear only where a 50 digit correct kernel can
decide them: where the true value rounded to 50 digits sits exactly
on the format grid (the pure small argument cases), only the nearest
modes are emitted, because the directed decision needs an enclosure
across the rounding seam — that is fd-aqs.7's contract, tracked
separately. The localized directed mode fixes (mode aware
overflow/underflow gates, negation reflected constant rounding,
tanh saturation) landed in the same arc one commit earlier
(fd-aqs.5).

## Consequences

- The ADR-0032 proof envelope's error model — kernel error small and
  *relative* at every input — holds again by construction: no
  derivation subtracts quantities large against its result, and no
  formula feeds an anchor absorbed sum whose information loss is
  absolute. The band corpus pins the class; the original review
  witnesses all round correctly against libmpdec/mpmath.
- ADR-0033's universal Decimal32 statement ("proven correctly
  rounded at every canonical input") is re-scoped: the exhaustive
  Arb margin search and the 18 vector kernel gate stand unchanged,
  but the inference from margin to every input relied on the error
  model this ADR repairs, so between 2026-05-28 and this change the
  statement was false in the anchor bands. It is now believed true
  again on the repaired model; re-running the exhaustive kernel
  verification over the canonical Decimal32 anchor decades (an
  offline campaign in the ADR-0033 Plan C4 mold) would restore the
  statement to *verified* rather than *modeled* and is left open.
  The per function rustdoc no longer states the universal claim
  without naming this ADR's scope note.
- The sampled corpus minima and the exhaustive worst case margins
  quoted in the per function rustdoc are unchanged: the mid domain
  derivations were not touched, except `acos`, whose new form is the
  same function computed through `atan` (the frozen corpus, the
  exhaustive gate, and the metamorphic suite pin the values).
- The directed mode residue for grid exact small argument results is
  named, bounded (1 ULP, nearest modes unaffected), and tracked as
  fd-aqs.7 rather than hidden.
- `docs/testing.md` gains the band corpus as a standing layer; its
  blind spot (a finite committed corpus, like the Arb layer's) is
  covered by the exact per bucket pins and the generator's margin
  guard.

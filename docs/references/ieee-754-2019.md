# IEEE Std 754-2019

The standard behind the crate family's name and contract. The load
bearing clauses, as cited across the code and ADRs: §3 storage formats
and BID encoding (ADR-0001), §3.5.2 canonicalization and DPD, §5
required operations including §5.4.1 FMA and §5.10 totalOrder, §5.7.2
sameQuantum and the quantum operations, §6 special values and the
§6.2.3 NaN propagation rule, §7 exceptions and the §7.4 underflow and
CLAMPED semantics (ADR-0048), §9.2 recommended correctly rounded
functions (ADR-0032), and §9.6 magnitude minimum and maximum
(ADR-0028).

Paywalled, so the registry records the document number and DOI rather
than a URL, and the entry can never be vendored or archived. The
lineage entries (ieee-754-2008, ieee-854-1987) record where the
decimal story entered the standard; the proxy literature entries make
the contract reconstructible without buying the document.

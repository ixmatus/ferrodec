# Payne and Hanek 1983

Payne-Hanek reduction is the standard answer to trigonometric
argument reduction at extreme magnitudes, and the reason ferrodec's
`sin`/`cos` hold their correctly rounded contract out to the format
boundaries instead of degrading past a few periods of 2 pi. The
implementation derives from the published windowing idea plus the
spec's precision requirements (code provenance discipline: the paper
is the idea's source, the implementation is the project's own); the
`U512` wide integer exists in `ferrodec-multiword` precisely to carry
the windowed product. The ADR-0033 exhaustive trig scan to full emax
confirmed the analytic reduction error bound empirically.
